# Editorial imagery

Created with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-09. Original PNG outputs are
stored in `public/images/editorial/`; Next.js serves responsive optimized versions.
These images illustrate service care and planning. They do not identify real staff,
customers, catalog products or completed work.

## Final prompts

### service-still-life.png

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium FieldOps field-service website hero, panoramic 3:2 image. Create a photorealistic sculptural still life of precision service tools: a beautifully machined chrome open-end wrench diagonally crossing a folded dark emerald green work cloth, a small steel hex nut, and a slim brass screwdriver. Background is a rich deep forest green matte architectural surface, softly lit with a broad shaft of natural window light from upper left, creating graceful long shadows. Art direction: high-end industrial editorial product photography, tactile material detail, sophisticated restraint, generous dark negative space across the right half for website overlays. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks, no UI, no people. Avoid blue, neon, gradients, plastic toy appearance. Tools physically coherent, realistic metal and woven cloth. Wide landscape composition.

Premium architectural magazine photography, authentic material texture, calm mood, generous uncluttered upper third.
